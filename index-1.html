How to use with Pocket Option
1. Select the same asset and timeframe visible on Pocket Option.
2. Generate/import candles that correspond to that chart.
3. Run the scan near the close of a candle rather than acting on an unfinished candle.
4. Compare the displayed CALL/PUT/WAIT setup with your own chart before taking any action.
5. The scanner does not log into Pocket Option, click trades, submit orders, or guarantee an outcome.
